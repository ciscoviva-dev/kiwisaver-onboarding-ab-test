# Results summary

Variant B lifts sign-up conversion from 39.54% to 42.55% (+3.01%, 95% CrI +1.65% to +4.37%), with a 100.0% probability of being the better arm. That is worth about 2,048,647 NZD in 5-year NPV at a staged 25% rollout (~15,713 members/yr annualised). But B also raises post-selection abandonment from 10.8% to 14.6% and 14-day fund-switch regret from 4.2% to 9.0% (it *improves* fund fit). Recommendation: Stage the rollout to 25% with a confirmation-step reassurance, then re-evaluate before scaling to 50% and 100%.

# Results summary

Variant B lifts sign-up conversion from 39.85% to 43.15% (+3.30%, 95% CrI +1.94% to +4.67%), with a 100.0% probability of being the better arm. That is worth about 2,176,643 NZD in 5-year NPV at a staged 25% rollout (~17,259 members/yr annualised). But B also raises post-selection abandonment from 10.8% to 14.8% and 14-day fund-switch regret from 4.2% to 9.0% (it *improves* fund fit). Recommendation: Stage the rollout to 25% with a confirmation-step reassurance, then re-evaluate before scaling to 50% and 100%.
